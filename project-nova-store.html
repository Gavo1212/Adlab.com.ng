The Challenge

The previous store had too many steps between browsing and checkout, and a lot of visitors were dropping off before completing a purchase.

The Approach

We simplified the store down to a focused product catalogue, a one-page checkout, and clear shipping information up front, paired with launch ad creative to drive initial traffic.
